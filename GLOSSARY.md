# Project D.O.T.

An autonomous IoT biodome that synthesizes soil and keeps the biome healthy for crops in space.

## Language

**Pod**:
One modular acrylic growing chamber with its sensors and drip system.

**Biome**:
The soil, plants and environment inside a pod.

**Injection I(t)**:
Nutrient actuator command.

**Uptake U(t)**:
Nutrient consumed by crops.

**Probing signal**:
Small pseudo-random addition to I(t) that keeps the loop identifiable for calibration.

**Instrumental variables**:
Estimation trick using the previous sample to remove noise bias.

**Demo mode**:
Dashboard running the model in the browser with no backend.
